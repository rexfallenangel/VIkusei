# Game translation frame

- Theme: modern-day domestic romance around a young woman who becomes a VTuber; her older cousin is the only one who knows her real face.
- Setting: present-day Japan, a shared apartment; livestreaming, SNS, part-time work and household routines.
- Era / technology: contemporary smartphones, streaming PCs, webcams and social media; no fantasy or sci-fi elements.
- Overall English register: casual contemporary English. Narration is first-person male, direct and sensual; dialogue is conversational. Adult content is explicit and blunt, not euphemistic.
- Naming policy: Hepburn romanization for Japanese names; 栞梨 = Shiori, しおりん = Shiorin (distinct names for the same person, different personas). 主人公 is the unnamed player character; use "Protagonist" for the nameplate and "I/me" in prose.
- Honorifics: keep only the source-supported Onii-chan address (see quirks); otherwise use natural English address rather than stacking honorifics.
- Folklore / mythology: none.
