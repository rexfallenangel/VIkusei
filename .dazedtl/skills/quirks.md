# Translation quirks

- 栞梨 and しおりん are the same person but distinct personas. Keep **Shiori** for the real cousin and **Shiorin** for the stream persona; never merge the names or let the stream voice bleed into off-stream scenes. In persona lines, keep the cutesy ～ and ♡ where the source has them.
- Shiori calls the protagonist お兄ちゃん. Render it as **Onii-chan** consistently, including in heated or joking lines; do not replace it with "big brother", "bro" or "brother". Other characters do not use it.
- The protagonist is male, uses 俺, and narrates in first person. Do not turn his narration into second person, and do not attach a name the source never gives him.
- Preserve the source's punctuation pauses: `……`, `！？`, `♡`, `♪`, and full-width spaces (`　`) that pace a line. Do not collapse them into plain ASCII or delete them.
- リスナーさん is an audience address, not a person named "Listener". Use "viewers" or "chat" as the sentence needs; keep the streamer's public voice warm and performative.
- Keep the H terminology blunt and consistent: 中出し = creampie, 射精 = ejaculation, 絶頂 = climax, イク = cum, 手コキ = handjob, 手マン = fingering, アナル = anal. Do not soften these into vague euphemisms.
- Gauges read upward: 好感度 (Affection) and 依存度 (Dependency) rising is good; do not write "recovers" for a drop. 体力 (Stamina) and 所持金 (Money) are resources.
- The source sometimes uses a full-width space between clauses for breath; keep a comparable pause rather than running the words together.
- Never add speaker names, quotation marks or explanations that are not in the source. UI labels stay short and title-cased like the glossary.
