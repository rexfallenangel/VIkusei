# Game glossary — V_ikusei (VTuber cousin)

# Characters
栞梨 (Shiori) - Female; the protagonist's younger cousin and live-in VTuber. This is her real, off-stream name. Blunt and guarded at first, gradually warm; calls the protagonist お兄ちゃん; casual endings, stutters when flustered (う、うそ), soft えへへ when pleased. Do not merge with her stream persona.
しおりん (Shiorin) - Female; Shiori's on-stream VTuber persona. Cutesy, playful stream voice with ～ and ♡; calls viewers みんな; refers to herself as しおりん. Keep distinct from 栞梨 (Shiori): the public face belongs to viewers, the real Shiori to the protagonist.
主人公 (Protagonist) - Male; the player character, Shiori's older cousin, first-person 俺. Calm, teasing, often the one proposing escalating situations. Nameplate label 主人公 renders as "Protagonist"; in running text use "I/me" naturally.
おば (Aunt) - Female; the protagonist's aunt, Shiori's mother figure. Minor speaking role in the intro.
